# Credits

- **Sustainable development goals.** The goal titles and target texts are
  those of the United Nations, General Assembly resolution A/RES/70/1 and the
  global indicator framework, as served by the UN Statistics Division SDG API
  (https://unstats.un.org/sdgapi).
- **Aurora SDG queries v5.0.** Vanderfeesten, M., Otten, R. and Spielberg, E.
  _Search Queries for "Mapping Research Output to the Sustainable Development
  Goals (SDGs)"_. Aurora Universities Network. v5.0 (2020),
  <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3817445>. CC BY 4.0. Source of the target
  lists of the impact questions.
- **Definitions of the technologies.** The definitions of the six advanced
  digital technologies (`adt_descriptions_v3.md`) were written for this
  research and follow, in paraphrase with a few short phrases kept, these
  sources:
  - artificial intelligence: OECD, Recommendation of the Council on
    Artificial Intelligence; Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act), Article 3(1)
    and recital 12;
  - Internet of Things: ITU-T Recommendation Y.2060 (2012); ISO/IEC 20924;
    OECD (2018), IoT measurement and applications;
  - blockchain: ISO 22739; NIST IR 8202, Blockchain Technology Overview;
  - robotics: ISO 8373:2021, as quoted by the International Federation of
    Robotics; UNCTAD, Technology and Innovation Report 2021;
  - additive manufacturing: ISO/ASTM 52900:2021.
